# Verity Dash

A 2D game that is heavily inspired by Geometry Dash. Your verity keeps moving forward on its own and you have to time your jumps to get through the level without dying. If you die you start over from 0%

# Where to play?
You can play it on itch.io here: (i still didnt export the game)


## About

You get to control a verity that cant stop moving forward. Try to beat the levels (currently there is only one level) without dying because if you do then well... you need to start over. You can also control the verity in a ship mode!!! And also very cool stuff like jumpads and jump orbs


## Controls

Space or left click to jump (In ship mode you hold to go up and let go to fall).
Esc to pause the game
In the level select use the left and right arrows buttons to switch levels and click the ribbon to play

## Stuff in the levels

* blocks that are solid so you can stand over them and stuff but make sure not to crash into one or else... YOU DIE

* spikes that kill you if you touch them

* jump pads launch you high into the air when you touch them.

* jump orbs also launches you into the air but only when you jump on them

* portals so you could change your mode


## Progress Bar

At the top of the screen theres a bar that shows how far you are in the level as a percent with a label under it that also shows you the percent but in text!


## Level Complete Screen

After you finish a level you get a very cool fade in animation you will see how many attempts it took you to beat the level and you will have 2 choices, either restart the level or go back to the main menu


## Level Select

You slide between levels like in Geometry Dash. The background changes for every level and the card shows the name of the level and your best percent (even tho there is only 1 level currently).


## Pause Menu

Press Esc and the game blurs and a window pops in with resume, restart and menu buttons. When you resume it does a 3, 2, 1 countdown first so you arent instantly thrown back into the level.


## Saving

If you accidently leave the game then dont worry because your best progress is saved for each level!!!
